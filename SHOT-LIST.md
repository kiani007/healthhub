# Shot list

45 photos and videos for the ten Tasting Board mockups: 10 hero videos and 35 photos.
Generate each with its prompt, save it with the file name shown, and send it over. Start with the hero videos (marked ★).

**Send files:** attach them in chat, upload them to `incoming/` in this repo, or share a Google Drive folder.
They get compressed, cropped, given poster frames and wired into each page's `MEDIA` slots.

**Every shot:**
- Videos: 1080p MP4, 6–10 s, slow steady camera, clean loop, no sound needed.
- Photos: JPG/PNG, at least 2000 px on the long side, in the ratio shown.
- No people, hands, faces, text, logos, watermarks or floating bokeh circles. Regenerate anything with warped cutlery or fake writing.

Append to every video prompt: _Photorealistic and cinematic, slow steady camera, seamless loop, 1080p, no audio. No people, no hands, no faces, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no floating bokeh circles._

Append to every photo prompt: _Photorealistic editorial food photography, natural textures, sharp focus, high resolution. No people, no hands, no faces, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no floating bokeh circles._
