(06)Since 1978 ۱۹۷۸ سے

ذائقہ جو نسلوں سے چلا آ رہا ہے

A taste handed down, one kadhai at a time.

  1. 1978Haji Abdul Qadeer opens a nine-foot shop on Bank Road with one kadhai and a brass tarazu.
  2. 1991The jalebi counter starts. Iftar queues reach the bank next door.
  3. 2004A bakery oven arrives: cake rusk, nan khatai and chicken patties.
  4. 2019Satellite Town opens and the third generation takes over the kitchen.
  5. TodayStill cooked from 4 am, still weighed on the same scale.
