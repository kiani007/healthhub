    06 Questions & inquiry

    Tell us about your day

    How far ahead should I book?

    Five days for one- and two-tier cakes, ten days for three tiers, two weeks for four tiers, and three weeks for any wedding cake. Peak months (December and the two Eids) fill a month ahead.

    How is the price worked out?

    By weight and finish: buttercream from Rs 4,200 per kg, semi-naked from Rs 3,800, fondant from Rs 5,600. Premium sponges, gold leaf and sugar flowers add to that. The builder above gives a close estimate.

    Do you deliver and set up?

    Yes, across Islamabad and Rawalpindi in a chilled van. Tiered cakes are stacked at the venue. Delivery starts at Rs 2,500; Murree and Bahria Town are quoted separately.

    Can you match a dress or a theme?

    Send a photo of the fabric or invitation and we will mix the buttercream to match at the design stage.

    Eggless or gluten-free?

    Eggless sponges are available in vanilla, chocolate and lotus. We are not a gluten-free kitchen, so we cannot promise a cake safe for coeliac guests.

    What if I need to change the date?

    Deposits move with you once, free, if we have the new date open. Cancellations within 7 days of the event keep the deposit.
