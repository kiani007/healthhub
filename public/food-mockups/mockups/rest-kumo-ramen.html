(01) Build the bowl 作り方

Seven layers, built in under a minute

Every bowl is built in the same order, at the counter, in front of you. The broth takes eighteen hours. The rest takes about fifty seconds.

Step 07 / 07Ready

  1. 01

    Broth スープ

    18 h

    Beef bones boiled hard for eighteen hours until the broth turns milky, then ladled over shio tare at 98°C.

  2. 02

    Noodles 麺

    55 s

    Thin, low-hydration noodles cut every morning. Boiled fifty-five seconds and folded in by hand.

  3. 03

    Chashu チャーシュー

    6 h

    Beef brisket rolled, braised six hours in soy and ginger, and torched just before it goes on.

  4. 04

    Ajitama 味玉

    6 m 30 s

    Eggs boiled six and a half minutes, then marinated twelve hours in soy, kombu and black tea. Jammy yolk every time.

  5. 05

    Nori 海苔

    1 sheet

    Toasted seaweed set against the rim so it stays crisp for your first bite.

  6. 06

    Scallions ねぎ

    At service

    Spring onion sliced thin when your ticket comes in, never before.

  7. 07

    Chilli oil ラー油

    2 or 20 drops

    House rayu with Kashmiri chilli, garlic and sesame. You decide how much.

All halal. Our tonkotsu-style broth is made only from beef bones. No pork, no mirin, no alcohol anywhere in the kitchen.

(03) How to visit 来店

Five ways to get a bowl

The counter is walk-in only. If there's a line, put your name down from anywhere and we'll text you when you're two parties away.

カウンター

Fourteen stools, no reservations

Come in, take a stool when one opens, order at the machine. It's a ramen counter, so most guests eat and go in about forty minutes.

  • Groups of up to 6 sit together; bigger groups split
  • 45-minute seat limit on Friday and Saturday nights
  • Solo diners usually get in fastest
