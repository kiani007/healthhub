About Aurelia

We cook thirty-two dinners a night from what reached Islamabad that morning: trout from Swat, morels from the Kaghan valley, apricots dried on Hunza rooftops. Nothing is rushed. The evening takes as long as it takes.

(01) The menu

Seven courses, one evening

The menu turns with the season and the valleys it comes from. Every course has its own pairing, poured from teas, shrubs and house sodas. The meal takes about two and a half hours.

  1. 01Amuse

    Margalla Morning

    A chilled yoghurt sphere on pine-nut crumble, finished with sumac and a few drops of apricot-kernel oil.

    Pairing Cold-brewed green tea, lemon verbena

  2. 02Cold

    Hunza Apricot & Goat Cheese

    Sun-dried Hunza apricots, whipped goat's cheese from a farm near Murree, toasted walnut and young beetroot leaves.

    Pairing Apricot-kernel and ginger shrub

  3. 03River

    Trout from Swat

    River trout cured for six hours in salt and ajwain, with pressed cucumber and a pool of dill buttermilk.

    Pairing Cucumber, mint and house tonic

  4. 04Forest

    Gucchi & Saffron

    Wild morels foraged in the Kaghan valley, a saffron rice cracker and brown butter whisked with aged ghee.

    Pairing Smoked Kashmiri chai, unsweetened

  5. 05Fire

    Charcoal Quail

    Quail grilled over kikar charcoal and lacquered in tamarind, with burnt onion petals and a roomali crisp.

    Pairing Tamarind and black-lime soda

  6. 06Slow

    Lamb Shank, Ten Hours

    Lamb shank braised overnight, nihari jus reduced for ten hours, roasted bone marrow and garden herbs.

    Pairing First-flush Darjeeling, served warm

  7. 07Sweet

    Falooda, Undone

    Rose kulfi, soaked basil seeds, a crisp vermicelli tuile and pistachio dust. Everything falooda is, taken apart.

    Pairing Rose and cardamom milk tea

(02) Experiences

Four ways to spend the evening

Tasting Room 32 guests Chef's Counter 8 seats Private Salon Up to 14 Pairing flight 7 pours

Tasting Room

Rs 18,500 per guest2.5 hoursTables for 2 to 6

The main room seats thirty-two at well-spaced tables under low brass light. Courses arrive together for each table, so conversation sets the pace, not the kitchen.

  • Seven courses plus bread service with cultured makhan
  • Two seatings: 6:30 pm and 9:15 pm
  • Window tables face the Margalla hills, ask when you book

Chef's Counter

Rs 24,000 per guest3 hours8 seats

Eight stools along the pass. The chef plates in front of you and introduces each course, with two extra bites that never reach the dining room.

  • Nine courses, including two counter-only bites
  • Kitchen tour before dessert
  • Best for 1 to 4 guests; the whole counter can be booked

Private Salon

Minimum spend Rs 260,000Up to 14 guestsOwn entrance

A panelled room with one long table, its own entrance and a dedicated server. Used for nikkah dinners, milestone birthdays and board dinners. The menu is written with you two weeks ahead.

  • Custom menu and printed place cards
  • Lunch bookings possible on weekdays
  • Enquire by phone: +92 51 555 0147
Enquire about the Salon

Pairing flight

Rs 6,500 per guest7 poursAlcohol-free

Seven pours built for each course: single-estate teas, fermented shrubs and sodas made in house from fruit, spice and bark. Add it to any seating.

  • Served in small glasses, about 90 ml each
  • Caffeine-free version on request
  • Can be added at the table
